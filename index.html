🟣 unfollow-studio – Take Back Control of Your Feed

unfollow-studio is a free, local-first application that gives you a clean, easy-to-read interface for managing your Instagram followers and following list. Instead of tapping through profiles one by one, you get everything in one organized view, right on your own computer. There is no backend server, no sign-up, and no password sharing — the tool runs entirely on your machine and only works with the data you choose to load into it.

⬇️ Download unfollow-studio

Visit this link to download the application.

✨ What unfollow-studio Does for You

Most people have no idea who actually follows them back. Over months and years, a following list quietly fills up with inactive accounts, old acquaintances, and profiles that never returned the favor. unfollow-studio turns that messy list into something you can actually read and act on.

You get a simple page with your followers on one side and your following on the other. The tool then highlights the accounts that appear in one list but not the other, so you can spot non-followers instantly. From there you can decide what stays and what goes — at your own pace, with no automation running in the background and no third party watching.

Because everything happens in your browser, the application is fast. There is nothing to wait for in the cloud, no queues, and no monthly limits. Open it, look at your lists, make your choices, and close the tab when you are done.

🔍 Main Features

  • Non-follower detection. Compare your followers and following lists side by side and instantly see which accounts do not follow you back.
  • Following management in one place. Browse your full following list in a readable layout instead of scrolling through the Instagram app.
  • Follower overview. Keep a clear record of who is currently following you and how your list changes over time.
  • Friendly visual interface. A clean, modern design built for regular users — no command line, no configuration files, no complicated setup screens.
  • Local-first by design. Your lists never leave your computer. There is no server to upload to and no database holding your data.
  • No account needed. You do not create a login, you do not hand over a password, and you do not connect a third-party service to your profile.
  • Runs in your browser. Built with plain JavaScript, so it works in the browser you already use every day.
  • Free and open. No subscription, no trial timer, no locked premium tier.

🔒 Privacy Comes First

Privacy is the whole point of this project. unfollow-studio is a client-side application, which means the code runs on your computer rather than on a remote server. Nothing is transmitted back to the developer, because there is no backend to transmit to.

You keep full control of what you load into the tool and when you clear it out. Closing the page ends the session, and your information stays exactly where you put it — on your own device.

🚀 Getting Started on Windows

Getting the application onto your computer takes just a few moments. You do not need any programming knowledge, and you do not need to install extra software.

⬇️ Get unfollow-studio

Visit this link to download the application.

  1. Open the download page. Click the button above, or type the address github.com/Slime2r1r31/unfollow-studio into your browser's address bar and press Enter.
  2. Download the application. On the page, look for the download option and save the file to a folder you will remember, such as your Desktop or Downloads folder.
  3. Wait for the download to finish. You can see the progress in your browser's download bar. Larger files may take a minute or two depending on your internet connection.
  4. Open the downloaded folder. Press the Windows key, type "Downloads", and press Enter. Your file will be waiting there.
  5. Launch the studio. Open the application's main page in your browser and the interface will appear, ready for you to use.

If Windows shows a security prompt when you open a downloaded file, choose the option that lets you continue. This is a standard Windows message shown for files downloaded from the internet.

🖱️ Using the Interface

Once the page is open, the layout guides you through the whole process step by step.

Step 1 — Load your followers. Add the list of accounts that currently follow you. The studio displays them in a tidy, scrollable panel.

Step 2 — Load your following. Do the same for the accounts you follow. Both lists now sit side by side, easy to compare.

Step 3 — Review the results. The tool marks the accounts that do not appear in both lists. These are your non-followers — the accounts taking up space in your following list without returning the gesture.

Step 4 — Make your choices. Go through the highlighted accounts and decide which ones to remove. Take your time; there is no timer and no limit.

Step 5 — Clear your session. When you are finished, close the tab. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

💡 Practical Tips

  • Work in small batches. Reviewing twenty or thirty accounts at a time keeps the task manageable and lets you think about each one.
  • Keep a note of anyone you want to re-follow later. It is easy to forget a name once it leaves your list.
  • Check back every few months. Following lists change slowly, so a regular review keeps things tidy without any daily effort.
  • Refresh your lists before each session so the studio is always working with current information.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The page looks empty or broken. Make sure you opened the correct file from the downloaded folder. Try closing the tab and opening it again.

Updates are not appearing. A normal browser refresh usually solves this. Press F5 on your keyboard or click the refresh arrow next to the address bar.

The download did not start. Return to the download page, refresh it, and choose the download option again. Check that your browser is not blocking downloads from the site.

Text looks too small or too large. Hold the Ctrl key and press the plus or minus key to change the zoom level in your browser.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost anything? No. unfollow-studio is completely free to download and use.

Do I have to give my Instagram password? No. The tool never asks for your login details and does not connect to your account on your behalf.

Where is my data stored? On your own computer, and only for as long as the session is open. There is no cloud storage and no server database.

Does it run on Windows? Yes. It runs in the web browser on your Windows computer, so no separate installation is required.
